CDC Outbreak Agent

Transparency for the tool-grounded Ask agent, twice-daily DuckDB refresh, nightly evals, and forecast backtests. For research, education, and situational awareness—not clinical decision-making or official CDC guidance. Figures are epidemiology-week surveillance, not live case counts.
