Respiratory Virus Dashboard

Built for clinicians, coordinators, and researchers who need this week’s CDC respiratory picture—fast. Public CDC open data, an Ask agent that shows its sources, twice-daily refresh, and nightly answer-quality scores. Outlooks lead with last-week persistence; more complex models are experimental.

For research, education, and situational awareness—not clinical decision-making or official CDC guidance. Figures are epidemiology-week surveillance, not live case counts. Status chips below are engineering/QA notes about this site—not disease alerts.

Eval scores

These scores measure whether the Ask bot’s numbers match the database—not whether illness is rising or falling. They are answer-quality checks for reviewers. Low grounding scores mean a QA regression on the bot, not a clinical emergency.

Forecast backtest

—

Error by forecast horizon (weeks ahead). Lower is better. Last-value is the product primary; models that lose are experimental.

Case study

Ask QA (grounding): a stale-table warning once included a day count (14) that never came from a tool result. Nightly answer-quality scoring then marked many answers as ungrounded (for example 16.7% vs a 100% baseline). The numbers in the surveillance tables were unchanged—this was a bot-caption bug. Warnings are now number-free; scores here refresh on the next nightly after deploy.

Forecasts: backtests show complex models rarely beat repeating last week on these sparse weekly series—so outlooks lead with last-week persistence and label the rest experimental.

Live demo: the free hosted API sleeps when idle. Ask may take about a minute to wake; that is a hosting delay, not missing CDC data.
